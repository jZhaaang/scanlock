## What Scanlock is

Scanlock is a free, unofficial Reddit bot that replies to comments and posts containing `[[double bracketed]]` Deadlock item or ability names with the stats for that item or ability.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Valve Corporation or Reddit, Inc. Deadlock and all related names and data are the property of Valve Corporation. Game data is supplied by [deadlock-api.com](https://deadlock-api.com), which is likewise unaffiliated with this bot.

## Accuracy

Deadlock is a game in active development. Its data changes frequently and sometimes without notice, and the upstream source may itself be incomplete or out of date. Every reply names the game build it was drawn from and the date it was fetched. Scanlock is offered for reference only; do not rely on it where accuracy matters.

## Use

Scanlock replies to public comments and posts in subreddits where a moderator has installed it. Moderators may remove its comments and may uninstall it at any time. Do not use Scanlock to spam, to harass, or in any way that breaks Reddit's Content Policy or a subreddit's rules; a subreddit doing so may have the app removed.
